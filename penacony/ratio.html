Dr. Ratio

Acclaimed Scholar of the Intelligentsia Guild

"The most annoying thing about idiocy is that you can't explain it to an idiot."

Origin

Unknown


Faction / Affiliation

Intelligentsia Guild


Role in Story:

Dr. Veritas Ratio is a scholar from the Intelligentsia Guild, a faction funded by the Interastral Peace Corporation (IPC) that explores different worlds to collect knowledge. In Penacony, he plays a pivotal role in unraveling the mysteries of the Dreamscape and its influence on the planet's inhabitants. His extensive academic background and sharp intellect make him a valuable ally to the Trailblazer and their companions as they navigate the complexities of Penacony's altered reality. Dr. Ratio's contributions are instrumental in challenging the established order and aiding the team in their quest to restore balance to the planet.


Personality

Dr. Ratio is highly analytical and intellectually curious, always eager to uncover deeper truths and question established knowledge. He approaches problems methodically, valuing logic and evidence over emotion, which sometimes makes him seem detached or overly formal. Despite this, he shows a genuine dedication to helping others and is open to new ideas when presented with compelling reasons. His calm and composed demeanor often brings clarity to tense situations.


Relationships

Dr. Ratio largely operates independently, valuing logic and empirical understanding over emotional attachments. However, his alliance with the Trailblazer during the Penacony incident showcases a growing respect for their intuition and adaptability, contrasting with his own calculated methods. He also interacts with other members of the Nameless and the Penacony cast, often offering insight or critique rather than emotional connection. While he doesn't form traditionally close bonds, his intellectual partnerships—especially with those who challenge his thinking—are where his most meaningful relationships emerge.


Timeline


Arrival and Observations

Dr. Veritas Ratio, a distinguished member of the Intelligentsia Guild, arrives in Penacony during the Charmony Festival. Invited alongside other major factions, his presence is tied to the IPC's interests and his collaboration with Aventurine, an IPC executive. While not directly involved in frontline events, Dr. Ratio keenly observes the unfolding mysteries within the Dreamscape, offering insights rooted in his vast knowledge and analytical prowess.


Analytical Observations

Throughout the Penacony crisis, Dr. Ratio provides critical analyses that aid in understanding the Dreamscape's anomalies. His interactions, though limited, are marked by incisive observations that challenge prevailing narratives. Notably, he offers written encouragement to Aventurine, assisting him in escaping the Border of Nihility and leaving the Dreamscape.


Post-Crisis Reflections

Following the resolution of the Penacony incident, Dr. Ratio's role remains that of a reflective observer. He continues to analyze the events, drawing conclusions that contribute to the broader understanding of the Dreamscape's implications. His commitment to disseminating knowledge and combating ignorance underscores his actions and interactions.
