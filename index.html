Meet the Astral Express Crew!

Caelus

A cheerful and witty Trailblazer aboard the Astral Express. Caelus loves to crack jokes and bring smiles to the crew, even in the middle of danger. Though he can act nonchalant and playful, deep down he has a strong sense of responsibility. His lightheartedness often hides how deeply he cares about his companions and the worlds they visit.

Stelle

Spirited and endlessly curious, Stelle is the Astral Express’s lively Trailblazer. She’s known for her bold jokes, silly remarks, and the ability to find humor in even the strangest situations. Despite her often mischievous behavior, Stelle’s heart is big, and she stands strong for those she travels with, facing every new adventure with a grin.

About the Trailblazers

Although Caelus and Stelle are different in name and appearance, they are two sides of the same coin — different versions of the same protagonist. Whether you choose Caelus or Stelle, you'll experience the same story, the same friendships, and most importantly, the same fun-filled, goofy adventures across the galaxy!

Dan Heng

Dan Heng is the reserved but dependable archivist of the Astral Express. Though once distant, he has grown to trust and deeply care for his companions, forming quiet but meaningful bonds with each member. Calm and analytical, he often acts as the voice of reason during their travels. However, beneath his composed exterior, Dan Heng carries the heavy burden of his past life as Dan Feng — a legacy marked by tragedy and controversy. Determined to forge his own path, he strives to live not in the shadow of his former self, but as someone who protects and uplifts those he now calls family.

March 7th

March 7th is a lively, cheerful girl who travels aboard the Astral Express. Waking up with no memories of her past, she was given the name "March 7th" by the crew, after the date she was discovered frozen in six-phased ice. Despite the mystery surrounding her origins, she remains upbeat and full of curiosity about the world. March 7th carries a camera with her to capture precious moments, and she fights alongside her friends with her trusty bow and later a pair of swords bestowed upon her by her martial masters. Behind her playful demeanor lies a strong will and an unshakable loyalty to those she cares about.

Pom-Pom

Pom-Pom is the lively and enthusiastic conductor of the Astral Express. Always bustling with energy, Pom-Pom takes their duties seriously, making sure the train — and its crew — are well cared for. Despite their small, plush-like appearance, they have a big personality, often encouraging the crew with cheerful words (and the occasional playful scolding). Pom-Pom is deeply proud of the Astral Express and considers all its passengers part of one big, traveling family, fostering a warm and welcoming atmosphere aboard the train.

Himeko

Himeko is the intelligent and composed navigator of the Astral Express, often serving as a mentor and guiding figure to the younger members of the crew. She first discovered the Astral Express as a child and, through her curiosity and determination, helped repair it to its former glory. Calm under pressure and endlessly patient, Himeko inspires those around her to find their path among the stars. Though she projects a strong and independent front, there's a quiet comfort in knowing she’s never truly alone — Welt, a traveler from another world, seems to have chosen to stay by her side, as if silently promising to never let her face the cosmos without someone watching over her.

Pom-Pom

Welt Yang is the calm and wise elder of the Astral Express crew, often offering guidance shaped by countless battles and worlds he's experienced. Although he presents himself simply as "Welt," there are deeper truths to his past — a history tied to another universe and a different branch of the Imaginary Tree. Some who know of the legends might even whisper an older name, "Joachim Nokianvirtanen", though such stories are half-lost in the folds of distant realities. With a quiet strength and a steady hand, Welt protects his new companions, treating them as the family he chose after witnessing the fall of his original world.
