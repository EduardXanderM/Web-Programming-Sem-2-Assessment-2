Ruan Mei

81st Member of the Genius Society

"Without accelerating or postponing the arrival of death, life will always wither."

Origin

Unknown


Faction / Affiliation

Herta's Space Station


Role in Story:

Ruan Mei is a key member of the Genius Society and one of the core developers of the Simulated Universe, alongside Herta, Screwllum, and Stephen. Her expertise in life sciences and her elegant, approachable demeanor make her both a brilliant scientist and a calming presence. In the story, she assists the Trailblazer in navigating the Simulated Universe and provides critical insights into the nature of Emanators and Aeons. Though she operates largely behind the scenes, Ruan Mei plays a pivotal role in uncovering deeper cosmic truths and guiding the player through advanced simulations. Her work bridges scientific curiosity with existential discovery.


Personality

Ruan Mei is graceful, gentle, and deeply curious, with a calm and composed demeanor that puts others at ease. As a member of the Genius Society, she’s exceptionally intelligent—particularly in the field of life sciences—but never arrogant. She treats even the most complex topics with elegance and clarity, often explaining them with a warm smile. Despite her soft-spoken nature, she’s confident and unafraid to challenge ideas or take bold steps in her research. Ruan Mei values beauty, harmony, and knowledge, blending intellect with emotional insight. Her presence often feels like a comforting, thoughtful guide in the unknown.


Relationships

Ruan Mei is closely connected to fellow Genius Society members Herta, Screwllum, and Stephen, with whom she co-developed the Simulated Universe. She serves as the emotional and intellectual balance within this eccentric team, offering calm insight and empathy. Her genius earned her recognition from Nous, the Aeon of Erudition, leading to her induction into the Genius Society—an honor granted only to the most brilliant minds. She forms a gentle, guiding relationship with the Trailblazer, supporting their growth within the Simulated Universe. Though soft-spoken, those who work with her recognize the depth of her intelligence and compassion.


Timeline


Early Carreer and Rise to Prominence

Ruan Mei’s origin remains unknown, but she showed exceptional brilliance in the field of life sciences. Her tireless pursuit of biological truths and elegant approach to science earned her recognition across the cosmos.


Introduction to the Genius Society

Her intellect caught the attention of Nous, the Aeon of Erudition, who approved her entry into the prestigious Genius Society as Member #81—a title reserved for only the most extraordinary minds.


Simulated Universe Development

She joined forces with Herta, Screwllum, and Stephen to co-create the Simulated Universe, a vast virtual system designed to explore the natures of Aeons and the universe’s deeper laws. Ruan Mei contributed by shaping simulations around the origin of life and complex bio-behavioral phenomena.


Assisting the Trailblazers

Later, Ruan Mei appears as a guide and ally to the Trailblazer, offering thoughtful commentary and scientific support within the Simulated Universe. Her role helps unlock deeper layers of knowledge tied to Aeons and Emanators.
