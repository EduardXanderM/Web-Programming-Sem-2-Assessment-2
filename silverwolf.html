Silver Wolf

Reality Hacker

"Everything is just a game to me, and I’m good at it."

Origin

Punklorde


Faction / Affiliation

Stellaron Hunters


Role in Story:

Silver Wolf is a member of the Stellaron Hunters and one of the first characters introduced in the game. Alongside Kafka, she helps awaken the Trailblazer on the Herta Space Station and initiates the mission to implant the Stellaron. As a brilliant hacker, she plays a crucial role in breaking through digital defenses and systems, paving the way for the Hunters' goals. Though she prefers to stay behind the screen, her actions have real consequences across multiple story arcs.


Personality

Silver Wolf is clever, confident, and playful, often treating situations like a game. She has a rebellious streak and enjoys pushing boundaries, especially when it comes to technology and hacking. Despite her laid-back attitude, she’s fiercely loyal to her friends and deeply committed to the Stellaron Hunters' mission. Her sharp wit and quick thinking make her both unpredictable and invaluable.


Relationships

Silver Wolf shares a close partnership with Kafka, often working together during Stellaron Hunters’ operations. She respects Elio as their leader but tends to keep a bit of distance, preferring her own space. While mostly focused on her missions, Silver Wolf occasionally shows a protective side toward the Trailblazer and other allies, hinting at deeper connections beneath her cool exterior.
