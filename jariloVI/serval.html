Serval

Engineer of Sound and Sparks

"How can a rock star not have that kind of confidence?"

Origin

Belobog


Faction / Affiliation

Belobog, Silvermane Guards & Wildfire


Role in Story:

Serval is a former Silvermane Guard turned underground engineer and owner of the Neverwinter Workshop in the Underworld of Belobog. Though she left the military, she continues to fight in her own way—using her skills, connections, and rebellious spirit to support Wildfire and the Trailblazer. As the sister of Gepard and a close friend of Cocolia in the past, Serval stands at a crossroads between the old order and the future Belobog needs. Her involvement helps bridge the technological and social divide between the Overworld and Underworld, playing a key part in resisting the Stellaron’s influence.


Personality

Serval is bold, witty, and fiercely independent. She thrives on creativity and rebellion, often pushing boundaries with both her inventions and her ideals. Despite her laid-back and playful demeanor, she’s deeply compassionate and committed to helping those in need. Her confidence, charm, and electric energy make her stand out—whether she’s repairing machinery or rocking out on stage.


Relationships

Serval is the older sister of Gepard, and though they chose very different paths—hers rebellious, his dutiful—they still care for one another deeply. She was once a close friend of Cocolia, but their relationship fractured due to ideological differences, especially after Cocolia rose to power. Serval also supports Bronya, offering guidance and tools during her transition to leadership. Her connection to the Trailblazer is one of trust and cooperation, as she provides them with key aid during their mission in Belobog.


Timeline


Silvermane Dissenter

Once a member of the Silvermane Guards, Serval becomes disillusioned with Belobog’s rigid hierarchy and leaves the military. Her departure strains relationships with figures like Cocolia, but it marks the beginning of her independent path.


Overworld Engineer

Serval sets up the Neverwinter Workshop in the Overworld, using her technical skills to repair machinery, build devices, and support those marginalized by the ruling class. She secretly aids Wildfire and the resistance movement from this base.


Ally of the Trailblazer

When the Trailblazer arrives on Jarilo-VI, Serval provides crucial tools, guidance, and support to help uncover the truth behind the Stellaron crisis.


Reconciling the Past

As tensions rise, Serval faces the consequences of her past ties to Cocolia and reconnects with her brother Gepard, helping to bridge divides despite their differing loyalties.


New Beginnings

With Bronya leading Belobog, Serval continues to contribute her expertise and hope, playing a vital role in rebuilding a more just and unified society.
