Sampo

Mr. Cold Feet

"All sorts of business are welcome — as long as you've got the cash."

Origin

Belobog


Faction / Affiliation

Belobog, Wildfire & Masked Fools


Role in Story:

Sampo is a mysterious and elusive figure who appears at critical moments to save the Trailblazer and their companions from danger. Though he rarely stays long, his timely interventions and combat skills prove vital in helping the group survive the threats on Jarilo-VI. His true motives and origins remain unclear, adding an intriguing layer of mystery to the story.


Personality

Sampo is calm, reserved, and enigmatic, often keeping his thoughts and emotions hidden. He speaks little but acts decisively when it matters most. His aloof nature makes him hard to read, but those who earn his trust know him as a reliable and skilled ally with a quiet sense of honor.


Relationships

Sampo keeps most people at a distance, revealing little about his past or personal connections. He shows a quiet respect for the Trailblazer, often appearing at crucial moments to assist them. His interactions with other party members are brief and limited, reflecting his solitary nature and tendency to act independently.
