Bronya

The Supreme Guardian of Belobog

"This place is always part of our homeland, even when it has been corroded by Fragmentum. Silvermane Guards will never turn their backs on their home."

Origin

Belobog


Faction / Affiliation

Belobog & Silvermane Guards


Role in Story:

As the next Supreme Guardian of Belobog, Bronya begins her journey as a dutiful leader upholding the ideals of the Overworld. Tasked with overseeing the Trailblazer’s arrival, she soon uncovers the harsh truths hidden beneath the city’s surface. Her growing disillusionment leads her to defy her mother, Cocolia, and join the Trailblazer in the fight against the Stellaron’s corruption. Bronya’s choices are instrumental in bridging the divide between the Overworld and Underworld, and her eventual rise to leadership sets Belobog on a new path toward unity and change.


Personality

Calm, composed, and highly responsible, Bronya carries the weight of leadership with quiet strength. She is intelligent and empathetic, often putting others before herself. While initially loyal to her family and duties, she possesses a strong moral compass that drives her to question authority and seek the truth. Her journey reveals a growing courage and determination to protect those she cares about, even if it means defying tradition.


Relationships

Bronya shares a complex bond with her mother, Cocolia, whose rigid rule she ultimately challenges. Her friendship and growing trust with the Trailblazer inspire her to reconsider her loyalty to Belobog’s regime. She also has a close, evolving relationship with Seele, whose passion and rebellious spirit influence Bronya’s transformation. Her interactions with Gepard, her loyal protector, reveal a softer, more vulnerable side beneath her composed exterior.


Timeline


Heir of Belobog

Born and raised to be the Supreme Guardian’s successor, Bronya is trained to uphold Belobog’s laws and maintain its image. She begins her journey firmly loyal to her mother, Cocolia, and the Overworld’s rule.


Meeting the Trailblazer

Bronya is assigned to escort and monitor the Trailblazer upon their arrival on Jarilo-VI. Through their interactions, she starts to see cracks in the city’s perfect facade.


Awakening and Doubt

Witnessing the hardships of the Underworld and the resistance’s cause shakes Bronya’s loyalty. She questions the city’s divided society and her mother’s harsh methods.


Defection and Rebellion

Choosing truth over duty, Bronya secretly aids the Trailblazer and Wildfire in their battle against Cocolia and the Stellaron’s influence. Her rebellion marks a turning point in the conflict.


New Supreme Guardian

With Cocolia defeated, Bronya assumes leadership, aiming to heal Belobog’s divisions and build a future where Overworld and Underworld coexist in peace.
