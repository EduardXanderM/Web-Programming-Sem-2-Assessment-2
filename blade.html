Blade

The Ghost of the Past

"When will death come for me? My patience is wearing thin."

Origin

Xianzhou Alliance


Faction / Affiliation

Stellaron Hunters


Role in Story:

Blade is a member of the Stellaron Hunters and a formidable swordsman who has transcended his mortal form, pledging loyalty to "Destiny's Slave." He possesses a terrifying self-healing ability, making him a relentless force in battle. Throughout the narrative, Blade's actions and motivations remain shrouded in mystery, adding layers to his complex character.


Personality

Blade is stoic and reserved, rarely revealing his emotions. He carries a deep sense of duty and honor, driven by his mysterious past. Despite his cold exterior, he shows a strong commitment to his cause and those he deems worthy. His calm and composed demeanor masks a fierce warrior spirit and unyielding resolve.


Relationships

Blade’s connections are few and often distant due to his reserved nature. He maintains a professional and sometimes tense relationship with other Stellaron Hunters, respecting their skills but keeping his own distance. His bond with the Trailblazer is complicated, marked by occasional cooperation and underlying mistrust. Despite this, he occasionally shows subtle care toward allies who earn his respect.


Timeline


The Herta Space Station

Blade is introduced as a formidable member of the Stellaron Hunters. He assists Kafka in awakening the Trailblazer and implanting the Stellaron, setting the stage for the protagonist's journey .


Jarilo-VI - "Embers of a Wildfire"

Blade's presence is felt as the Stellaron Hunters' plans unfold in the background. His direct involvement remains minimal during this arc, but his influence is evident as the story progresses.


Xianzhou Luofu - "Vanguard of the Hunt"

Blade confronts Dan Heng, revealing that he is the reincarnation of Dan Feng, a powerful exiled Vidyadhara criminal. This revelation adds complexity to Dan Heng's identity and motivations. Blade's actions during this arc are pivotal in uncovering hidden truths and advancing the narrative .
