Origin

Unknown


Faction / Affiliation

Astral Express & Stellaron Hunters


Role in Story:

Caelus and Stelle are the player-named protagonists of Honkai: Star Rail, known collectively as the Trailblazer. They awaken aboard the Herta Space Station with no memories, carrying within them a mysterious and dangerous entity called a Stellaron. Despite their unknown origins, they join the Astral Express crew and begin a journey across the galaxy, helping resolve crises caused by Stellarons on various planets. As they travel, they uncover the truth about Aeons—powerful god-like beings—and begin to forge their own path among the stars, growing stronger and more connected to the universe's secrets.


Personality

Caelus and Stelle carry the weight of the stars with quiet grace and unshakable resolve. They are fearless explorers—calm yet fiercely determined—whose curiosity burns like a beacon in the cosmic darkness. Guided by empathy and a deep sense of purpose, they inspire hope wherever their journey takes them, forging bonds that transcend worlds.


Relationships

Throughout their journey, the Trailblazers—Caelus and Stelle—form deep, lasting bonds with their closest companions aboard the Astral Express: March 7th, Dan Heng, Himeko, Welt, and the ever-spirited conductor Pom-Pom. On Jarilo-VI, they forge strong alliances with Bronya and Clara, standing beside them to free the planet from the shackles of its frozen past. In the Xianzhou Luofu, they gain the respect of figures like Jing Yuan and Fu Xuan, and face morally complex connections with Kafka and Blade, who seem tied to their origins. Their time in Penacony introduces strained but significant relationships with Black Swan, Firefly, and Sparkle, where friendship and uncertainty intermingle within the illusions of the Dreamscape. By the time they reach Amphoreus, the Trailblazers have become a unifying presence—one that brings people together, challenges fate, and inspires change across every world they touch.


Timeline


The Herta Space Station

The Trailblazer awakens with no memories, implanted with a Stellaron by Kafka. They are introduced to the world of Stellaron Hunters and soon join the Astral Express crew after helping stabilize the station.


Jarilo-VI - "Embers of a Wildfire"

The crew travels to the snow-covered world of Jarilo-VI, uncovering a history of oppression and the truth behind the Supreme Guardian. The Trailblazer helps Bronya and Clara bring change to Belobog and defeats Cocolia, resolving the Stellaron crisis.


Xianzhou Luofu - "Vanguard of the Hunt"

Aboard the massive Xianzhou Flagship, the Trailblazer gets entangled in political conflict, Stellaron corruption, and the threat of the Disciples of Sanctus Medicus. They confront Blade, Kafka, and unravel connections to their past, while allying with Jing Yuan and Fu Xuan.


Penacony - "The Planet of Festivities"

Invited as guests of honor, the Trailblazer explores the surreal Dreamscape of Penacony. They encounter the Family, Black Swan, and Firefly, uncovering disturbing truths behind the Dreams and the lingering influence of the Stellaron. The line between illusion and reality blurs as the crisis deepens.


Amphoreus - "Flamechase Journey"

The Trailblazer arrives on Amphoreus, an oceanic and lore-rich world. The narrative begins to reveal deeper cosmic forces, the mysteries of the Aeons, and the Trailblazer’s evolving role in shaping the fate of the galaxy.
